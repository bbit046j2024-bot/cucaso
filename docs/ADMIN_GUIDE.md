# CUCASO Platform Officer Administration Manual

Welcome to the CUCASO Platform Administration Guide. This manual provides clear, step-by-step instructions for executive officers, chapter representatives, and central committee members.

---

## 1. Council Members & Super Admins

### Reviewing Chapter Applications
1. Log in at `/portal` and ensure your 2FA code is entered.
2. Select **Council Applications Queue**.
3. Review the prospective institution, patron endorsement document, and chapter leadership contacts.
4. Cast your vote: **Approve**, **Request Changes**, or **Reject**.
5. Once a simple majority (>50%) approves, assign the official **Capability Tier** (Tier 1 through Tier 4).

### Managing Capability Tiers
- **Tier 1 (Weight 2.0x)**: Large universities (e.g. TUM, Pwani University).
- **Tier 2 (Weight 1.5x)**: Medical training colleges, regional campuses (e.g. KMTC Mombasa).
- **Tier 3 (Weight 1.0x)**: Technical polytechnics and mid-size colleges.
- **Tier 4 (Weight 0.5x)**: Secondary school SDA fellowships and newly chartered groups.

---

## 2. Central Treasurer

### Configuring Rally Budgets & Locking Fees
1. Navigate to `/portal` -> **Finance & Cost Engine**.
2. Enter the rally's **Fixed Costs** (Venue lease, sound system, tents, security).
3. Set the **Per-Head Rate** (Meals, registration badges, handbooks per attendee).
4. Configure **Contingency Reserve** (Default 10%).
5. Review the automated capability-weighted allocation table. Note how cross-subsidies support smaller colleges.
6. When the registration deadline arrives, click **Lock Rally Fees & Generate Invoices**.
   *Note: Locking freezes weights and issues official invoices across all 12 chapters.*

### Reconciling M-Pesa & Bank Remittances
1. Navigate to **Payment Ledger**.
2. M-Pesa payments with matching chapter codes are automatically matched to open invoices.
3. For manual bank deposits or cash, click **Record Manual Payment**, enter the transaction reference, and attach the deposit slip image.

---

## 3. Chapter Representatives & Treasurers

### Registering Rally Delegates
1. Navigate to `/portal` -> **Chapter Attendees**.
2. Download the CUCASO CSV template or enter attendees manually.
3. For secondary school attendees under 18:
   - Ensure the guardian name and emergency phone are provided.
   - Upload the signed guardian consent confirmation.
4. Submit attendee list before the announced registration cutoff.

### Paying Chapter Rally Invoices
1. Open **Chapter Invoices**.
2. Locate the invoice number (e.g. `INV-2026-TUM-01`).
3. Follow the Safaricom Paybill instructions:
   - **Business Number**: `CUCASO Paybill`
   - **Account Number**: `[ChapterCode]-[RallyCode]` (e.g. `TUM-01-RAL-2026`)
4. Download the stamped PDF Official Receipt once matched.
